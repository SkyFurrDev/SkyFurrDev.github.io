# Furr
Furr is the social network for the furry community: a place to share your fursona, your art, and your fursuits, connect with like-minded people, and find your pack.


🐾 Your profile and fursonas
Build your profile with your species, pronouns, interests, links, and a gallery. Add all your fursonas—complete with their colors, traits, and personalities—and select your primary one.


📝 Post and discover
Share text, photos, GIFs, videos, and polls. Follow others, like, reply, repost, quote, and save content you enjoy. Explore trending topics and hashtags, and organize people into lists.


💬 Private and group chats
Chat privately with friends or create group chats. Messages from people you don’t follow arrive as requests, so you’re always in control.


👥 Groups and communities
Join public, private, or invite-only groups centered around your favorite interests—or create your own, complete with custom rules and moderators.


📅 Events and meetups
Discover local meetups and conventions, RSVP, and add them to your calendar. Organize your own events, whether online or in person.


📍 People nearby (optional)
Choose to share your approximate location to find other furries in your area. We never display exact coordinates, and you decide who can see you and how much detail they can view.


🔒 Privacy and security
Enjoy features like private accounts, control over who can message or mention you, visibility settings for your age, blocking and muting tools, human-reviewed reports, and the ability to download your data. Sensitive content is hidden by default.


🌍 Available in Spanish and English.


Furr is free to use and is exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older.
